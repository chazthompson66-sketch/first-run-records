FAQ

THE QUESTIONS ARTISTS ACTUALLY ASK.

How long does vinyl pressing take?

Turnaround depends on plant capacity, specification, artwork approval and shipping. We quote a realistic production window against your required release or tour date before you commit.

Can I order only 50 records?

Yes. Short-run 50-copy options are a core part of the First Run model. The exact specification available at 50 copies can vary by pressing partner.

What files do I need to provide?

We will need production-ready audio and artwork. The exact formats depend on the pressing and print specification, and we will give you a checklist before production.

Can you do coloured vinyl?

Usually, yes, subject to the chosen plant and run size. Coloured, marbled or recycled options can affect price and availability.

Can I change the merch mix?

Yes. The package names are starting points. We can adjust quantities and swap items depending on your audience, budget and tour length.

Are the profit figures guaranteed?

No. They are planning examples based on quantities and selling prices. Real results depend on how much stock sells and on fees, VAT, venue commissions, discounts and other costs.

Do you handle delivery?

Yes. The aim is a coordinated, tour-ready delivery. Final freight cost and delivery arrangements are confirmed as part of the quote.

What happens after I accept a quote?

We confirm the specification, collect approved production files, lock the order, coordinate manufacture and keep you updated through production and delivery.

Can you work with labels as well as individual artists?

Yes. The service is suitable for independent labels, bands, solo artists, DJs and producers who want manageable physical runs.

Is VAT included?

Quotes will state clearly whether VAT applies and whether it is included. The calculator on this site is illustrative and should not be treated as a VAT calculation.
