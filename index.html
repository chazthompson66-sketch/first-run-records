FAQ

THE QUESTIONS ARTISTS ACTUALLY ASK.

Can I order only 50 records?

Yes. 50-copy genuine vinyl pressing is one of our core options, alongside a 100-copy package.

Can you press more than 100 records?

Absolutely. We specialise in smaller runs but can quote 250, 500, 1,000+ copy pressings. Tell us the quantity and specification and we’ll price the right production route.

What is included in the vinyl-only price?

Our standard 50 and 100 packages include 12" 140g black vinyl, full-colour labels, white inner sleeves, full-colour printed outer covers, shrinkwrap, 3 test pressings, VAT and UK delivery.

How long does vinyl pressing take?

Turnaround depends on plant capacity, specification, artwork approval and shipping. We confirm the expected production window before you commit.

What files do I need to provide?

We need production-ready audio and artwork. We’ll give you a clear checklist before production.

Can you do coloured vinyl?

Usually, yes. Coloured, marbled or other specialist options are quoted separately because they can affect price and availability.

Can I add merchandise?

Yes. Choose one of our vinyl + merch packages or ask us to tailor the mix for your release or tour.

Do you work with labels?

Yes. We work with independent labels, bands, solo artists, DJs and producers.
