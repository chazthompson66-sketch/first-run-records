We take the messy bit out of physical releases and tour merchandise. You deal with one point of contact while we coordinate the manufacturing, artwork checks and delivery.

01

Send us your release date, tour dates, quantities and the merchandise you want. We shape the package around the size of your audience and budget.

BUILT FOR INDEPENDENT ARTISTS

WHY FIRST RUN?

Most independent bands do not need thousands of records or a maze of manufacturing accounts. They need sensible quantities, clear costs and somebody to coordinate the job properly.

First Run Records brings vinyl and tour merchandise into one managed order. The idea is deliberately straightforward: you make the music and artwork; we organise the physical product, production and delivery.

Our focus is on smaller, commercially useful runs for artists who want something they can actually sell — at gigs, through their own store, to fans and around a release — without tying up unnecessary cash in huge quantities.

THE FIRST RUN PROMISE

  • Clear quote before anything goes into production
  • Realistic small-run quantities
  • Vinyl and merchandise coordinated together
  • Production files checked before manufacture
  • No inflated order size just to suit a factory minimum
  • One point of contact from quote to delivery
