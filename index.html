FAQ

THE QUESTIONS ARTISTS ACTUALLY ASK.

Can I really order only 50 records?

Yes. Genuine 50-copy vinyl pressing is one of our core services, alongside our 100-copy option.

Can you press more than 100?

Absolutely. We specialise in smaller runs but can quote 250, 500, 1,000+ copies.

What is included?

Our standard packages include 12" 140g black vinyl, full-colour labels, white inners, full-colour printed outer covers, shrinkwrap, 3 test pressings, VAT and UK delivery.

Can I add merchandise?

Yes. We can quote printed T-shirts, tote bags, posters, stickers, badges, slipmats and other tour or release merchandise.

Can you do coloured vinyl?

Usually, yes. Coloured, marbled and specialist options are quoted separately.

How long does pressing take?

Turnaround depends on specification, approvals, plant capacity and shipping. We confirm the expected production window before you commit.
