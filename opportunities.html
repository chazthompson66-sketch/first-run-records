LIVE SALES TARGETS

Prioritise people with a reason to buy now — upcoming gigs, tours, releases or funding.

Seed list uses public industry/event sources and is designed to be expanded and re-qualified before outreach. Do not treat an event listing as permission for bulk unsolicited marketing.

TOUR OUTREACH

“Saw you’ve got dates coming up. First Run puts the vinyl and complete merch table together as one order — and we can show the potential merch-table return before you commit.”

RELEASE OUTREACH

“Saw you’ve got new music coming. We help independent artists turn a release into a physical product — short-run vinyl plus the merch around it, coordinated in one place.”

VENUE / PROMOTER REFERRAL

“First Run helps the artists you book arrive with a proper merch table: vinyl, tees, totes, posters, stickers and badges. We’d like to give your artists a direct tour-package route and track referrals back to you.”

REFERRAL OFFER — WORKING MODEL

Give each venue/promoter a unique QR/source code. Track enquiries and won orders. Agree any referral reward only after supplier margins are confirmed.
