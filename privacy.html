FIRST RUN RECORDS

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 15 September 2026

First Run Records respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and use personal information when you contact us, request a quote, submit an enquiry, or provide your details through our website or advertising lead forms.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide to us, including your name, email address, telephone number, artist or band details, project requirements, requested quantities, timescales, and other information you choose to include in an enquiry.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, discuss vinyl pressing or merchandise requirements, provide customer service, and administer our relationship with you. We may also use information to understand the effectiveness of our advertising and improve our services.

Facebook and Instagram lead forms

If you submit your details through a Meta (Facebook or Instagram) lead form, the information you provide is shared with First Run Records so that we can respond to your enquiry. We use that information for the purposes described in this policy.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers or production partners where reasonably necessary to provide a requested service, obtain a quotation, fulfil an order, operate our website, or comply with legal obligations.

How long we keep information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, maintaining business records, and meeting applicable legal, accounting, or tax requirements.

Your rights

Depending on applicable UK data protection law, you may have rights over your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Contact us

If you have a question about this privacy policy or how First Run Records uses your information, contact us at hello@firstrunrecords.co.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy when our services or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page.
